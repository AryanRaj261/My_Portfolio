# Product Requirements Document

## Product

Aryan Raj Portfolio is a single-page professional portfolio that helps recruiters, collaborators, and potential clients quickly understand Aryan's frontend focus, relevant work, experience, and contact options.

## Primary users

- Recruiters reviewing candidates quickly.
- Hiring managers assessing frontend craft and technical judgment.
- Potential collaborators looking for a direct contact route.

## Goals

- Establish Aryan's frontend identity within the first screen.
- Make selected work and documented projects easy to scan.
- Show motion and implementation skill without misrepresenting third-party studies as client work.
- Provide a direct email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and résumé path.
- Work reliably on desktop, mobile, keyboard-only, and reduced-motion contexts.

## Core experience

1. A visitor lands on a clear hero with a selected-work and email call to action.
2. They can review concept case studies through project tabs.
3. They can review résumé-backed builds, skills, credentials, and experience.
4. They can use the contact section or the client-side portfolio guide to reach relevant information.

## Non-goals

- The portfolio guide is not a real-time AI service and must not collect or store messages.
- Source-integrated studies are not client case studies.
- Public project links must not be invented or redirected to unrelated destinations.

## Success criteria

- The primary work and contact routes are discoverable without scrolling through every section.
- Each portfolio item accurately states its context, contribution, stack, and available evidence.
- Core navigation and work selection pass browser tests on desktop and mobile.
- The production build, lint, and source-integrity verification pass before deployment.
